STAKEHOLDER ANALYSIS: BPO Support Ticket Optimization

Understanding the audience for this analysis ensures the final Tableau dashboard and SQL insights are tailored to actual business needs, rather than just abstract metrics.

1. EXECUTIVE STAKEHOLDER: VP of Customer Success / Director of Operations
- Primary Goal: Improve overall Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) and reduce customer churn.
- Core Interest in Data: The financial and experiential cost of SLA breaches. They need the "big picture."
- Required Deliverable: High-level executive presentation highlighting the correlation between SLA failures and CSAT drops, along with strategic recommendations for headcount or channel investments.

2. TACTICAL STAKEHOLDER: Support Operations Manager / Floor Supervisor
- Primary Goal: Optimize daily team performance and hit SLA targets.
- Core Interest in Data: Granular bottleneck identification. They need to know exactly which channel (Email, Chat, Phone) is falling behind and what times of day have the worst resolution rates.
- Required Deliverable: Interactive Tableau dashboard allowing them to filter ticket volume and average handling time (AHT) by channel, priority, and day of the week to adjust staffing shifts.

3. TECHNICAL STAKEHOLDER: IT & Routing Systems Administrator
- Primary Goal: Ensure the automated ticket routing systems are functioning correctly.
- Core Interest in Data: Priority routing integrity. They need to know if "Critical" tickets are actually reaching agents faster than "Low" priority tickets.
- Required Deliverable: SQL reporting outlining the Average Resolution Time specifically segmented by Ticket Priority level. 

COMMUNICATION STRATEGY:
- The raw SQL logs will be documented for technical review.
- The Tableau dashboard will be designed for the Tactical Stakeholders (Operations Managers) for daily use.
- The final Canva presentation will be distilled specifically for the Executive Stakeholder, focusing strictly on business impact and ROI(Return On Investment).
