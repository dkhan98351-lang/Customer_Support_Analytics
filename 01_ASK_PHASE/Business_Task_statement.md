# Business Task Statement : BPO Customer Support Optimization

## 1. Background and Context
The company has observed a recent stagnation in Customer Satisfaction (CSAT) scores and an increase in escalated support tickets. Management suspects that certain communication channels are creating bottlenecks and that Service Level Agreements (SLAs) are being consistently breached, but they lack the data-driven visibility to pinpoint the exact operational failures.

## 2. The Business Task
Analyze 200,000+ historical customer support tickets to identify operational bottlenecks, calculate the financial impact of SLA breaches, and provide actionable staffing and routing recommendations to improve overall CSAT scores.

## 3. Key Stakeholders
* **VP of Customer Success:** Needs high-level strategic insights to improve overall customer retention and satisfaction.
* **Support Operations Manager:** Needs granular data on channel performance and priority routing to optimize weekly staffing schedules.

## 4. Key Questions to Answer
To solve the business task, this analysis will answer the following specific questions:
1. **Channel Efficiency:** Which support channel (Email, Chat, Social Media, etc.) handles the highest volume, and which struggles with the highest Average Resolution Time?
2. **The SLA Impact:** How drastically do CSAT scores degrade when a ticket resolution breaches a 48-hour SLA compared to when it is met?
3. **Routing Integrity:** Are "Urgent" and "High" priority tickets actually being resolved faster than "Low" priority tickets, or is the internal routing system failing?
4. **Product Bottlenecks:** Which specific products or customer segments generate the highest volume of critical issues?

## 5. Expected Deliverables
* A documented SQL data cleaning and transformation log (Google BigQuery).
* A comprehensive analysis report answering the key business questions.
* An interactive Tableau dashboard designed for operations managers to monitor SLA breaches and CSAT in real-time.
* A high-level executive presentation with finalized business recommendations.
