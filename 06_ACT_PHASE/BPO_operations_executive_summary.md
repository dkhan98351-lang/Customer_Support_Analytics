# ACT PHASE: BPO Operations Executive Summary

## Project Overview
An end-to-end data audit was conducted on over 200,000 historical customer support interactions. The primary objective was to evaluate compliance with the 48-hour Service Level Agreement (SLA), measure its impact on Customer Satisfaction (CSAT), and identify systemic bottlenecks across the BPO floor.

## The State of Operations: Critical Failures
The data reveals a department in an operational crisis. The failures are not isolated to specific agents or teams; they are systemic.

1. **Massive Operational Deficit (80% Breach Rate)**
   Exactly 80.08% of all closed tickets fail to meet the 48-hour SLA target. The floor is currently operating with a backlog of over 32,000 breached tickets.
2. **Universal Floor Bottlenecks (2.5x Delays)**
   Every single communication channel (Phone, Email, Chat, Web, Social) is averaging ~120 hours to resolve. Because all channels are failing equally, this indicates a severe, floor-wide headcount shortage.
3. **Inverted IT Routing System**
   Automated routing tags tickets as "Urgent", but they take the longest to resolve (121.1 hours). "Low" priority tickets are resolved faster (119.9 hours). This proves floor agents are bypassing the routing protocol and cherry-picking easier tickets.
4. **High-Value Revenue Risk**
   "Corporate" clients wait exactly as long as standard individuals (~120 hours). There is no expedited VIP protocol, placing our most lucrative contract renewals at immediate risk of churn.
5. **The CSAT Anomaly**
   Customer Satisfaction remains stagnant at 3.0/5.0 whether the 48-hour SLA is met or breached. This indicates that customers are fundamentally dissatisfied with the *quality* of the resolution, not just the speed.

## Strategic Mandate
The company cannot simply ask agents to "work faster" when the system averages 120-hour delays. We must immediately restructure the CRM routing logic to prevent cherry-picking, establish a dedicated VIP queue to protect corporate revenue, and initiate a budget review for increased headcount.
