# ACT PHASE: Strategic Implementation Plan

**Objective:** A phased, 90-day execution roadmap to resolve systemic bottlenecks, fix CRM routing failures, and restore SLA compliance across the BPO floor.

---

## Phase 1: Immediate Triage & Revenue Protection (Days 1–30)
*The focus of the first 30 days is stopping the bleeding by fixing broken software processes and protecting our highest-paying clients.*

* **Action 1.1: CRM Routing Audit & "Force-Routing"**
  * **Owner:** IT & Operations Management
  * **Directive:** Remove the UI capability for floor agents to manually browse the queue and cherry-pick easy tickets. Implement a strict "next-in-queue" automated push, forcing "Urgent" tickets to the front of agent screens.
* **Action 1.2: Establish the VIP Corporate Queue**
  * **Owner:** Workforce Management (WFM)
  * **Directive:** Immediately pull the top 10% performing senior agents off the general floor. Dedicate this team exclusively to the "Corporate" customer segment to ensure high-value contracts receive expedited, sub-48-hour resolutions.

## Phase 2: Structural Scaling & Quality Control (Days 31–60)
*Process fixes alone cannot bridge a 120-hour delay. Phase 2 addresses the fundamental lack of resources and agent quality.*

* **Action 2.1: Headcount Budget Review**
  * **Owner:** Finance & VP of Operations
  * **Directive:** The data proves a universal floor shortage (every channel averages 120 hours). Finance must approve an immediate hiring wave to scale up Tier 1 support agents across Email and Phone channels to clear the 32,000+ ticket backlog.
* **Action 2.2: CSAT Quality Retraining**
  * **Owner:** Quality Assurance (QA) & Training Dept
  * **Directive:** Address the "CSAT Anomaly" (scores stuck at 3.0 even when fast). QA must audit transcripts of tickets that met the SLA to find out why customers are still unhappy. Update the training manual to prioritize resolution *accuracy* over resolution *speed*.

## Phase 3: Monitoring & Optimization (Days 61–90)
*Ensuring the changes stick and tracking the ROI of the new implementations.*

* **Action 3.1: Live Dashboard Integration**
  * **Owner:** Data Analytics Team
  * **Directive:** Connect the Tableau `BPO_Operations_Dashboard` directly to the live CRM database. Mount monitors on the BPO floor so Shift Managers can track the live SLA breach ratio and channel bottlenecks in real-time.
* **Action 3.2: Re-Evaluate the SLA Target**
  * **Owner:** Executive Stakeholders
  * **Directive:** If the 48-hour target is mathematically impossible with the current budget, operations must transparently adjust the external customer expectation to 72 hours while internal scaling occurs, reducing customer frustration.

---

## KPIs for Success
To measure the success of this implementation plan, the Data Analytics team will track the following metrics weekly:
1. **Urgent Ticket Resolution Time:** Must drop below the "Low" priority average (Target: <24 hours).
2. **Corporate Segment Wait Time:** Must drop significantly below the overall average (Target: <48 hours).
3. **Overall SLA Breach Rate:** Reduce the 80% failure rate by 15% month-over-month.
