# SHARE PHASE: Presentation Speaker Notes

**Objective:** This document serves as the official transcript and speaker guide for the "BPO Operations Analytics" executive presentation.

---

**Slide 1: Title Slide**
> "Good morning everyone. Thank you for joining me. Today, I am going to walk you through a comprehensive analysis of our recent customer support performance data. Specifically, we are looking at our 48-hour Service Level Agreements (SLA) to uncover the root causes of our systemic bottlenecks."

**Slide 2: The Business Objective**
> "Our primary goal for this analysis was to figure out exactly why we are failing to meet our 48-hour SLA target. To do this, I analyzed over 200,000 historical support tickets. Our scope was to identify where the bottlenecks are occurring so we can design targeted routing adjustments and staffing strategies."

**Slide 3: Overall SLA Performance: The 80% Crisis**
> "I won't bury the lead. The data revealed a massive operational deficit. Looking at our closed tickets, 80% failed to meet the 48-hour SLA deadline. Out of roughly 40,000 completed tickets in this sample, over 32,000 breached the target. We are currently operating in a crisis state."

**Slide 4: Channel Bottlenecks: Systemic Floor Delays**
> "Our first step was to find out *where* the delay is happening. If it was just the Email team, we could retrain them. However, as this chart shows, every single channel is missing the 48-hour target by the exact same margin. Phone and Email are trapped at 120.7 hours, and even our 'rapid response' Chat is sitting at 119.8 hours. This proves we have a severe, floor-wide headcount shortage, not just a single bad department."

**Slide 5: Priority Routing: Inverted Resolution Times**
> "Next, we looked at our internal IT routing. We found a critical process failure. The system tags tickets as 'Urgent', but as you can see, 'Urgent' tickets actually take the longest to resolve—averaging over 121 hours. Meanwhile, 'Low' priority tickets are being resolved faster. This strongly indicates our agents are cherry-picking easier tickets from the backlog instead of handling emergencies first."

**Slide 6: High-Value Revenue Risk**
> "Finally, we looked at customer segmentation. This heatmap shows that our Corporate clients—our highest revenue tier—are waiting over 117 hours across all channels. They are waiting exactly as long as standard Small Business and Individual users. Because we have no VIP routing protocol in place, we are putting our most lucrative contract renewals at severe risk."

**Slide 7: Strategic Recommendations**
> "Based strictly on the data, I recommend three immediate actions:
> First, we must audit the CRM logic and implement 'Force-Routing' to stop agents from cherry-picking easier tickets. 
> Second, we need to pull 10% of our senior agents off the general floor to create a dedicated VIP queue to protect our Corporate revenue. 
> Third, because every channel is missing the SLA by 2.5x, process fixes alone won't work. We must initiate an immediate budget review to scale our general headcount."

**Slide 8: Thank You**
> "By trusting the data, we can fix these routing failures and protect our most valuable clients. Thank you for your time, I'd be happy to open the floor to any questions."
